A Close Look at the Partnership with the Department of Education

Behind the scholarships is an agreement with the Department of Education (DepEd) Region II. Each side has a clear job.

What ISA provides

  • Scholarships for students who need them
  • Mentorship, so scholars have support as well as funding

What DepEd does

  • Identifies the beneficiaries
  • Monitors their progress

Why the work is split this way

Scholars are identified by the people who know the schools and the learners, while ISA focuses on funding and mentoring. Each side does what it is best placed to do, and scholars benefit from both.
